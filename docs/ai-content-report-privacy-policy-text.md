## Reporting AI-generated content

AiFetchly lets you report AI-generated output that you believe is harmful,
illegal, deceptive, private, or otherwise inappropriate. The **Report AI
output** action appears next to user-visible content generated by AI, including
chat responses, generated images, plans, artifacts, email templates, keyword
sets, automatic replies, and website-analysis narratives.

### What we send

When you submit a report, AiFetchly sends to AiFetchly for developer review:

- **The selected AI output** — a bounded snapshot of the specific generated
  item you chose to report. Long text is truncated; generated images are
  re-encoded as small display previews (max 1024 pixels on the longest edge,
  max 1 MiB each, at most three images).
- **Your optional description** — up to 2,000 characters of explanation.
- **Source metadata for triage** — the surface where the output appeared
  (e.g. AI Chat, email-template editor), the content type, a stable message
  or artifact identifier, the conversation identifier when available, the AI
  model when available, your app version, your platform, your locale, and the
  generation timestamp when available. A stable install identifier may be
  included to help correlate recurring issues.

### What we do not send

We do **not** include your prompt, the full conversation, AI reasoning or
chain-of-thought, tool arguments, tool execution logs, attachment contents,
workspace files, browser content, email inbox, authorization headers, or any
neighboring messages. Only the single output you selected is transmitted.

### Identity and access

Reporting uses your current authenticated identity when available. If you are
not signed in or your AI plan has expired, the backend applies its anonymous
reporting rules — **reporting is never paywalled** and remains available when
hosted AI is disabled. The report action never calls an AI model and never
consumes AI credits.

Reports are accessed by AiFetchly safety and support reviewers for the purpose
of triaging reported content and taking appropriate action. Report content is
retained according to the companion backend privacy controls.

### Your controls

Reporting is voluntary. You choose the concern category and may add an
optional description before submitting. Nothing is sent until you select
**Submit report**. Closing the dialog before submission sends nothing. You may
retry a failed submission; retrying with the same dialog does not create a
duplicate report.

A successful submission returns a stable `air_...` reference you can record for
your own tracking.
