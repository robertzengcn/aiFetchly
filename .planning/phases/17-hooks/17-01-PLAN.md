| T-17-03 | Information Disclosure | workspaceRootHash | low | accept | SHA-256 hash is for stable dedup keying across path moves (V6 partial), not secrecy; the plaintext rootPath is also stored for UX. No secret material hashed. |
| T-17-04 | Tampering | HookRegistry.replaceSource | medium | mitigate | Defensive shallow copies ({...h}) on insert prevent caller mutation from corrupting registry state (CLAUDE.md immutability). | T-17-SC | Tampering | package installs | n/a | accept | Phase 17 installs ZERO new packages (RESEARCH § Package Legitimacy Audit — all deps already in package.json). No slopcheck checkpoint needed this plan. |
